SwingTribe adverts for clubhouse screens

Three campaigns in four looks, for a 1920 x 1080 television in a clubhouse, a pro shop or behind a registration desk. Each campaign is a 24 second loop plus the four stills it is cut from. Everything is downloadable from this page.

Every campaign's code is its own, so scans can be told apart by campaign. Codes are printed into the artwork and cannot be re-pointed afterwards: a change means new files.
