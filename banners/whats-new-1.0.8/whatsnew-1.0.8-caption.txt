SwingTribe 1.0.8 is out.

- Hole prizes on the scoring screen: the sponsor, the number to beat, and one tap to claim. Birdie and eagle challenges score themselves.
- Scores entered without signal are saved and sent when you reconnect.
- Sign in with a WhatsApp code, and register from outside South Africa.
- Anyone in the field can add players and guests once the tee sheet is out.
- Course lists sort A to Z, with a Nearby filter and distances.
- Your stats now count club rounds, not just SwingTribe games.

Free on the App Store and Google Play: play.swingtribe.app/open
